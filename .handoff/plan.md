## Goal

Redraw every in-battle character (4 heroes, 15 regular enemies, 5 bosses, the Spiritcaller's ally wolf) so it matches the game's painted gothic artwork instead of looking like flat clip-art. The characters stay procedural Canvas 2D code (no image files). Three things change:

1. A shared "finish" pass renders each unit to an offscreen canvas and paints light, shading, texture and a soft edge over it, so every unit sits in the painted backdrop.
2. Every unit is redesigned with real anatomy, layered shading and thin palette-tinted edges, replacing the thick pitch-black outlines, boxy bodies and repeated diamond kneecaps.
3. Every unit gets a visible idle motion, a distinct attack pose and a distinct cast pose.

Combat, balance, data and UI do not change. A new `npm run sprites` tool renders a contact sheet and checks each unit automatically.

### Art direction (applies to workstreams W2, W3 and W4)

- Draw only in local unit space: feet on `y = 0`, the body facing +x (the caller mirrors enemies). Keep each unit's overall height within the `baseline.json` window that `npm run sprites` checks.
- Use `paintShape` for every body part. Do not call `gothicShape`, `leadLines`, `drawArmoredLeg` or `glowGem` in new code, and never use `INK` or `#000` as an outline.
- Outlines: the outer silhouette uses a dark shade of the unit's own palette at width 1.4–2.2. Interior detail lines use width ≤ 1.2 and alpha ≤ 0.6.
- Shading: every major part (torso, head, each limb, cloak, wings, weapon) gets a top-to-bottom gradient fill plus a `shade` sub-shape on its lower or back side and a `light` sub-shape on its upper or front plane, so at least three tones per part. Metal gets a thin bright specular streak; cloth gets 2–4 fold lines.
- No diamond or gem kneecaps on anyone. Glowing gems only where a design below names them.
- Silhouettes: organic curves (quadratic segments), tapered limbs, distinct heads, hands and feet. No unit may be a single rectangle, triangle or gem shape with eyes.
- Animation reads `time`, `u.uid`, `u.anim.attack` and `u.anim.cast` (each 0..1):
  - idle: breathing (torso rise of 1.5–3) plus at least one secondary motion with its own phase (cloth or tatter sway, tail, ears, floating particles, flicker);
  - attack: the body leans forward ≥ 8°, and the weapon, limb or jaw extends along +x, scaled by `u.anim.attack`;
  - cast: a limb or the head lifts, and a glow at the casting point grows by `u.anim.cast`.
- Use only `Math.sin` and `Math.cos` of `time` and `uid` for variation. Never call `Math.random` in sprite code.
- Keep each unit's palette (`u.colors` / `c.main`, `c.dark`, `c.glow`) as its colour identity.
- New helpers go in the workstream's own file. Do not edit `kit.ts` after step 3.

## Parallel work

After step 5, run these four workstreams in parallel, one subagent each. Give each subagent this plan's **Art direction** section, its own brief below and only its own files. **W1 — finish pass.** Owns `src/sprites/finish.ts` and `src/art.ts` (only inside `drawUnit`).
- `drawFinishedUnit` renders the raw unit (the stub's dispatch) into an offscreen canvas and draws the finished image back at the caller's transform:
  - Device scale `k = min(2.5, hypot(m.a, m.b))` from `ctx.getTransform()`, where `m` is the transform.
  - The offscreen covers local x −180..180 and y −340..40, at `k` pixels per unit. It is one canvas per `u.uid`, kept in a `Map` and resized only when needed.
  - Draw it back with `ctx.drawImage(off, -180, -340, 360, 380)`.
- Paint over the raw sprite on the offscreen canvas, in order:
  - (a) grounding: a `source-atop` linear gradient from transparent at y −70 to `light.ground` at alpha 0.55 at y 0;
  - (b) grade: a `multiply` fill of `light.accent` at alpha 0.10, then `destination-in` with a mask copy of the raw sprite;
  - (c) texture: a 128×128 grain pattern built once with a seeded LCG (grey values 112–144) drawn with `overlay` at alpha 0.16, then `destination-in` with the mask;
  - (d) rim light: a band on the top edge in `light.accent` blended 50/50 with `u.colors.glow`. Make it from the mask filled with that colour (`source-in`), minus the same mask shifted by (2.2·k, 2.6·k) px with `destination-out`, drawn onto the offscreen with `source-atop` at alpha 0.55.
- When drawing back, use `shadowColor 'rgba(4,2,8,0.6)'`, `shadowBlur 5*k` and offsets 0, and keep the caller's `ctx.filter` and `globalAlpha`.
- Cache: when `drawFinishedUnit` is called again with the same key, redraw the cached image without re-rendering. The key is `u.uid`, `time`, `u.alive` and every `u.anim` field, so `drawUnit`'s smear and main draws in one frame render once, and a changed pose never reuses a stale image.
- In `drawUnit`, leave the existing order and filters as they are.
- Acceptance: `npm run build` exits 0, and `npm run sprites` reports no `edge` or `opaque` failures caused by the finish pass.
